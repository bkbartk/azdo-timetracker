Logging Rules

New time must go on a Feature, User Story or Task under the epic; its hours still roll up to the epic in reports. Existing entries on Epics are kept and can still be edited.

Work Item Fields

Project / Client — the field each time entry takes its Project and Client from: the logged work item's own value, else the nearest parent's (up to the epic). The default looks for Custom.Project / Custom.Client.
Total logged hours — when set, every add, edit or delete recomputes this field from scratch on the logged work item and every parent up to the epic, so an epic's value is its own hours plus every descendant's. Leave disabled to turn it off.

After changing a field, go to Time Reports, widen the date filter to your full history and click Re-sync once to bring existing entries and totals up to date.
